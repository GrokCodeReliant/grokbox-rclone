Privacy policy

This policy applies to the grokbox-rclone Google OAuth application, used only by the owner of Grok Code Reliant studio.

What this app is

The app is used only by the owner to copy the owner’s own files between the owner’s Google Drive and the owner’s own computer via rclone. It is not a public service, and it is not offered to other users.

Data collection and sharing

No data is collected, shared, sold, or stored by any third party through this application. Google user data is only accessed by rclone on the owner’s devices, for the purpose of copying the owner’s own files.
